# Healthcare-Dashboard
📌 Project Overview
This project presents a fully developed Healthcare Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI, designed to analyse hospital performance across clinical, operational, and financial metrics.
The dataset contains 40,235 patients, £1.417B in total billing, and detailed breakdowns of medical conditions, insurance billing, admission trends, and patient flow.

The objective is to demonstrate how data analytics and business intelligence can support better decision‑making, improve patient outcomes, and enhance operational efficiency within healthcare environments.

📊 Dataset Summary
The dataset includes:

Total Patients: 40,235

Total Billing: £1.417B

Average Length of Stay: 16 days

Average Billing per Patient: £25,539

Billing by Insurance Provider

Patient Count by Medical Condition

Admission Trends (2019–2024)

Billing by Admission Type

📈 Key Insights
1. Financial Stability & Diversification
Billing is evenly distributed across major insurance providers, each contributing ~$279M–$287M.
This indicates strong financial diversification and predictable revenue streams.

2. Chronic Disease Burden
Conditions such as Arthritis, Diabetes, Hypertension, Cancer, Obesity, and Asthma each represent ~19–20% of total patients.
This reflects a multi‑disciplinary chronic‑care population requiring long‑term management.

3. Length of Stay (LOS)
The average LOS is 16 days, significantly above typical hospital averages (4–7 days).
This suggests complex cases, rehabilitation needs, or opportunities for improved care coordination.

4. Admission Trends
Admissions remained stable at 11k annually from 2020–2023, showing predictable operational demand.
The 2024 figure (4k) appears to be partial data.

5. Billing by Admission Type
Elective (£478M), Emergency (£466M), and Urgent (£474M) admissions contribute almost equally to total billing, indicating balanced utilisation of hospital services.

🧠 Strategic Recommendations
1. Reduce Length of Stay (LOS)
Strengthen discharge planning

Improve inter‑department coordination

Expand home‑care and outpatient services

2. Build Chronic‑Care Programs
Develop specialised pathways for diabetes, hypertension, arthritis, cancer, and obesity.

3. Improve Patient Flow
Separate emergency vs elective pathways

Enhance triage efficiency

Expand urgent care capacity

4. Invest in Preventive Care
Community screening

Lifestyle and nutrition programs

Digital monitoring tools

5. Implement Predictive Analytics
Forecast bed occupancy, staffing needs, high‑risk patients, and seasonal admission patterns.

6. Improve Data Integration
Ensure complete 2024 data

Integrate EHR + billing systems

Standardise coding and reporting
